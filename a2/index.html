Damon turns 11 🥳

Siberian husky, born 24 October 2015

Meet Damon

Meet Damon, a Siberian husky born on 24 October 2015. He joined my life when he was a tiny one-month-old puppy, and this October he celebrates his 11th birthday.

He is playful, funny, a little silly and always curious. New smells, new paths, new adventures: Damon is ready for all of them. But the best thing about him is his loyalty. He has been my best friend for 11 years.

Our adventures

Damon was born in Ukraine, and in 2019 we moved together to Finland. Since then we have travelled a lot. We love hiking, and Damon has been with me everywhere: from the Åland Islands and the southernmost point of Finland to the northernmost point, and further to Swedish Lapland and the Lofoten Islands in Norway.

For a husky, the north is the perfect place: cold air, snow, mountains and endless new paths to explore.
